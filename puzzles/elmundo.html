    How to play

    A mystery country is hiding on the map. There is no limit on guesses: keep going until you find it. Each guess lights up on the map, and the hotter the colour, the closer its border is to the mystery country.

    blue = far, dark red = very close
    green = you found it

    Each row shows the distance between the two closest borders (0 km means they touch, so look next door), an arrow pointing from your guess toward the answer, and a closeness percentage. Tiny islands are hard to see, so every guess also drops a coloured pin.

    Easy picks well-known countries, Normal the rest, and Hard the little island nations and microstates, mostly in Oceania. Stuck? Use a hint (3 on Easy and Normal, 2 on Hard), or tap any country on the map to read its name, which costs one hint too. You can also give up to see the answer.
