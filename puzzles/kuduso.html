How to play

Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box holds the numbers 1 to 9 once. Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by logic.

Tap a cell, then a number. Notes lets you pencil in small candidates; a right number clears that note from its row, column and box. A wrong number turns red and counts as a mistake, so use Erase to take it back.

Easy starts with 40 numbers given, Normal 32 and Hard 26. A hint fills one correct cell (3 on Easy and Normal, 2 on Hard). On a keyboard: 1 to 9, arrow keys, Backspace, N for notes, H for a hint.
