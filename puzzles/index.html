The five puzzles

Words

Wxrdie

Guess the hidden 5 or 6 letter word in six tries. Green, yellow and grey tiles guide you.

Play Map

El Mundo

Find the mystery country on the world map. Hotter colours mean closer borders, with distance and direction clues.

Play Logic

Kuduso

A 9 by 9 number grid with one answer you can reach by logic. Notes and hints included.

Play Numbers

Mapnitude

Slide to estimate a size or distance. The closer your answer, the more points.

Play Maze

What to check when you test

  • Each page opens and plays on your phone without sideways scrolling.
  • Sound is on by default; the speaker button in each header mutes it.
  • Reload mid-game: the puzzle should resume where you left it.
  • Journey: walk, jump, stomp a creature, hit a ? block, rest at the tent, then fight the castle boss.
  • Tell me anything that feels too hard, too easy or unclear. Difficulty numbers are first guesses.
