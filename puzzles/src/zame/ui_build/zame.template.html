How to play

Get from the green start to the gold exit. Swipe on the maze, tap beside your explorer, use the arrow buttons or the arrow keys. You run down a corridor until you hit a junction, a dead end or the exit.

Easy is a 10×10 maze, fully visible, no clock. Normal is 15×15 against a countdown. Hard is 20×20 in the fog: you only see 3 steps down the corridors around you, the places you have been stay on the map, and the clock is tighter. The clock starts on your first move.

Stars come from your steps compared with par, the shortest way out (every step counts, backtracking too). A hint lights the next 8 steps of the right way from wherever you stand: 3 hints on Easy and Normal, 2 on Hard. Out of time or out of patience, you can give up and see the route.
