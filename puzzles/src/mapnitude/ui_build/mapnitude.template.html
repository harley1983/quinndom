How to play

Easy is 3 rounds, Normal and Hard are 4. Each one asks for a number about a country: how many people live there, how big it is, how high its tallest peak is, or how far apart two capitals are. Drag the slider (or use the 2% buttons) and lock in your best estimate.

The slider is logarithmic: each tick is ten times bigger than the last, so the first job is getting the order of magnitude right. Points depend on how many times too big or too small you are, not on the raw difference. Up to 1,000 a round, and 60% of the total passes.

Easy asks about famous countries, so it wants you within 5% for full points. Normal digs deeper and forgives 8%. Hard is the obscure ones, so it forgives 15%. Stuck? A hint squeezes the slider around the answer but costs 25% of that round's points the first time and 50% the second. You get 3 hints a game on Easy and Normal, 2 on Hard.

Numbers are current-day figures: population is the latest (2025) UN/World Bank estimate, area is total area, and peaks are each country's highest point.
