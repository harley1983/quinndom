How to play

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. Pick 5 or 6 letters at the top. Each guess must be a real word.

  • Green: right letter, right spot
  • Yellow: right letter, wrong spot
  • Grey: not in the word

Example: the word is PLANT

P
A
R
I
T

P is in the right spot. A and T are in the word but elsewhere. R and I are not in it.

Stuck? Each word comes with 2 of each hint. Remove letters greys out 5 letters on the keyboard that are not in the word. Reveal a letter shows one letter in its right spot (dashed tile).

Words are common English words. Every puzzle is new and you can play as many as you like.
